The Fanbu Story

反哺: A Duty to our Parents

This is me, my wife (Dana), and her parents. They are in their 70s, don't speak much English, and don't use much technology (with the exception of taking photos & WeChat'ing their friends). As a result, they've always had to rely on Dana: the forms, the calls, the bookings, anything in English, anything with technology.

We were always happy to help, but everything changed when we had our first child. Suddenly, Dana found herself balancing being a mom, a wife, and a businesswoman, while also rediscovering who she is postpartum. Even though she wanted to, there weren't enough hours in the day to be proactive with her parents, especially as the world becomes increasingly complex.

That's why I built Fanbu. For Dana, she gets back time while keeping peace of mind as a devoted daughter. For her parents, they get to achieve their goals without feeling like a burden.

Fanbu is an old Chinese legend about young crows, now grown, flying back to feed their parents. To return the care they were given. This is what 反哺 (fǎnbǔ) means.

Crows are often seen as a bad omen, but this legend made them a symbol of devotion in Chinese culture. In fact, they’re given a second name: 慈乌 (cí wū), the kind crow.

Our parents immigrated to the US so that we could have a better life: in a language they couldn’t speak & a community they did not know. And now they are aging in it, a place that feels increasingly complex, where doctor appointments require 2fa and insurance forms require multiple phone calls. The systems were not built for them. And for us, it’s not an obligation, but our duty.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. Is Fanbu a real person?

    Yes! Every family gets a dedicated concierge (me for the pilot), who will know your parents & own complex end-to-end tasks like payment and coordination. We built an internal AI to pair with the human concierge & remove busy work (eg. waiting on hold). That allows our team to focus on you, your parents, and their specialized needs.

    2. Can't I just use an AI assistant for this?

    Fair question, and it’s where I started with my in-laws. AI assistants can own individual tasks, but Fanbu owns outcomes. Booking the appointment is a task; making sure Mom gets there, understands her results, and has her follow-up booked is the outcome. That is Fanbu relieving the mental load, end to end.

    3. How does Fanbu's pricing compare to other senior concierge services?

    Competitors currently have two models: (1) hourly ($40–80/hour), where the meter runs every minute, even the unproductive ones, like waiting on hold and (2) project-based (eg. insurance plan change), where costs can go up to thousands of dollars depending on the project. With Fanbu, we charge one flat monthly price. Big jobs and small ones included, no surprise bills, no wondering if a request is “worth it.”

    4. How do you handle passwords and sensitive information?

    We will always start with options that do not require handling sensitive information. For example, for patient portals or insurers, we will always ask to be approved as a caregiver so that (a) we can log in as ourselves and (b) we can be removed at any time you or your parents see fit. If a task needs something more sensitive, we will ask only for that task through an encrypted password manager and promptly delete it when you ask.

    5. Is there anything you don't do?

    Think of us as your family’s remote care coordinator. That means:

    1. We don’t give medical, legal, or financial advice. We’re not licensed to, but we’ll find the right professional and handle the scheduling, paperwork, and follow-ups.
    2. We don’t come into the home for hands-on care or companionship. But we’ll find, vet, and set up a trusted provider, so it all feels turnkey.
    3. We’re not an emergency service. In an emergency, always call 911 first, then let us know and we’ll take care of the follow-up.
